BEFORE YOU APPLY

Keep your finance conversation practical

  1. Prepare land and crop details, seasonal cost estimate and repayment plan.
  2. Compare interest, fees, collateral and repayment schedule with an authorised lender.
  3. Never share OTPs or pay an unofficial processing fee. Confirm schemes on official government portals.
